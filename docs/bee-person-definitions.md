# Bizzing Bee — words whose definition is a person (for the owner to review)

Found by Bizzing English (2026-10-03) in Bee's `words-full.js` at its working tip. A definition with a life-span in brackets — "(1877-1923)", "(born in 1931)" — or naming a dictator describes a PERSON, not the word. Bizzing English already filters these out of every question (`tools/import-bee.mjs` `plain()`); in Bee they still reach spelling lists with that definition. Cutting or re-defining Bee words is the owner's call (Bee app3.js `CORE_CUT` was signed off one by one), so nothing in Bee was changed.

Two kinds:

1. **Everyday words wearing a person's definition** — e.g. *begin* ("Israeli statesman…"), *mason*, *banks*, *brooks*, *polo*, *villa*, *wren*. These need the everyday definition back, not a cut.
2. **Bare surnames and names** — e.g. *hitler*, *stalin*. Candidates for `CORE_CUT` under Bee's own keeping rules.

## Most urgent

Most words in the next table are good words (*tyrant*, *despot*, *dictatorship*). These ones are not words to ask a child to spell, or carry a definition no child should read: **hitler** (Bee level 1), **Jew-free** (level 4), **Untermensch**, **Japanazi**, **homocaust**, **Mischling**, **Aryanize**, **goebbels**, **goring**, **speer**/**speers**, **mussolini**. Also odd at level 1–2: **begin** and **begins** are defined as Menachem Begin.
